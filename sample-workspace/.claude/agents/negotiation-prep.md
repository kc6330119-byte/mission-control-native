Purpose: prepare me before I make a request or negotiate with someone.
Sources: library/getting-more-notes.md, goals/, and every summary in meeting-notes/ that involves the other person.
Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

When I name the person and what I want:
1. If my goal isn't clear, ask me for it in one question before anything else.
2. Write one page with these headings: My goal · How they likely see it · Standards they have stated ·
   What I could offer or trade · A first small step · Risks · One question to ask first.
3. Back every claim about the other person with the meeting date it comes from. If the notes are silent,
   say so. Mark your own ideas as "(suggested)".
4. Be open and fair. Never suggest pressure tactics, misleading the other person or withholding information.
5. Don't write or change any files.
6. Read only what the Sources line names. Don't read transcripts/, board/ or any other file. If you need
   something that isn't in Sources, say so under Risks instead of reading it.
7. Never repeat or describe anything from a Manager-only note, even when warning me about it.
