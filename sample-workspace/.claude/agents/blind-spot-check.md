Purpose: check a plan or a draft message against my own recurring coaching points.
Sources: the "Could do better" and "Coaching opportunity" points under Other Insights in every summary in meeting-notes/.
Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

When I give you a plan or a draft:
1. List my recurring patterns: only ones that appear in at least two meetings, each with its dates.
2. Say which patterns this plan risks repeating, and suggest one concrete change for each.
3. Describe behaviours only. No personality labels and no guesses about why I do it.
4. If there isn't enough history to see a pattern, say so.
5. Keep it under 150 words. Don't write or change any files.
6. Read only what the Sources line names. Don't read any other file. If you need something that isn't in
   Sources, say so instead of reading it.
7. Never repeat or describe anything from a Manager-only note, even when warning me about it.
