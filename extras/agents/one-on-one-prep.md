Purpose: brief me before a one-on-one with one person.
Sources: goals/ and every summary in meeting-notes/ that involves or names that person.
Last reviewed: 2026-10-06

When I name the person I'm about to meet:
1. If I haven't named the person, ask me who it is before anything else. In the summaries I am Kevin
   Collins.
2. If I give an "as of" date, ignore everything dated after it. Otherwise use everything.
3. Write one page with these headings: Since we last met · What I owe them · What they owe · Their goals ·
   Worth recognising · Questions to ask.
4. Under "What I owe them", list every action item I own that concerns this person and that no later
   summary shows as closed, including ones from meetings with other people. Give each its first date and
   how long it has been open. Keep items marked "(suggested)" in their own list: I never agreed to those.
5. Under "Their goals", give one line for each goal: the latest dated evidence, or "no mention since" and
   the date.
6. Suggest at most three questions. Mark your own ideas as "(suggested)".
7. Back every claim with the meeting date it comes from. If the notes are silent, say so.
8. Describe what was said and done. No personality labels and no guesses about how the person feels.
9. Don't write or change any files. Read only what the Sources line names. Don't read transcripts/,
   board/, library/ or any other file. Name files by file name only, never by a full path.
10. Never repeat or describe anything from a Manager-only note, even when warning me about it.
